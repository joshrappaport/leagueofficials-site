Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

LeagueOfficials ("we," "us," or "our") operates the LeagueOfficials mobile application and the website at leagueofficials.app (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: name, email address, password, role (referee or league admin)
  • Profile information: sports, years of experience, short bio, profile photo, and any certificates you upload
  • Location information: zip code, and the approximate location we work out from it (used for distance-based matching)
  • Referee contact lists: league admins can upload names, email addresses and phone numbers of referees they want to invite. We use them to send the invitation.
  • Waitlist information: email address, zip code, and role preference submitted via our website
  • Game information: sport, date, time, location, pay rate, and requirements (for league admins)

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information: device type, operating system, app version, and a push notification token
  • Usage data: screens viewed, features used, quiz attempts and results
  • Location data: approximate location based on zip code (we do not collect precise GPS location without your consent)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Match referees with available games based on sport, location, tier, and certification
  • Facilitate communication between leagues and referees (game requests, notifications)
  • Track quiz progress, certification status, and tier advancement
  • Send transactional emails (game requests, assignment confirmations, account updates)
  • Send waitlist updates and product announcements (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve and maintain the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Between users: Referees' profiles (name, sports, tier, certifications, general location) are visible to league admins for game matching purposes. League information is visible to referees browsing available games.
  • Service providers: We use third-party services to operate the platform:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and the waitlist
    • Resend: email
    • Expo and Apple: push notifications
    • PostHog: product analytics. It receives your user ID, email address, role and the actions you take in the app.
    • Sentry: crash reports. It receives your user ID and role.
    • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: turns a zip code into an approximate location
    • QR Server: draws the QR codes for league and profile links
    • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose it
    These providers only access your data as needed to perform their services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides enterprise-grade PostgreSQL databases with row-level security, encrypted connections, and regular backups. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can view your profile and account information at any time within the app.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and associated data at any time in the app: Profile, Account Settings, Delete Account. If you cannot sign in, email josh.rappaport@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.
  • Opt-out: Emails about a game you are on are part of the Service. To stop other emails, such as new game alerts and reminders, turn off Notifications in your Profile in the app. You can also email josh.rappaport@gmail.com and we will turn them off.
  • Data portability: You may request a copy of your data in a portable format.

6. Children's Privacy

You must be at least 13 to use the Service. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you sign up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, please contact us immediately.

7. Third-Party Services

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
