For Officials

Open the Home tab to see open games near you that match your sports. Tap a game to see the details, then tap Claim Game. You are confirmed at once. The league is notified and the game appears in My Games.

Whistle Tiers show how many games you have completed on LeagueOfficials.

  • Bronze Where everyone starts
  • Silver 50 games
  • Gold 150 games
  • Platinum 250 games
  • Diamond 500 games

Your tier moves up on its own as leagues mark your games complete. Rules quizzes in the Training tab earn Knowledge Badges (Rookie, Skilled, Expert) for each sport. Badges show on your profile. They do not change your tier.

First, check your spam or promotions folder — transactional emails occasionally land there. Emails are sent from games@leagueofficials.app; adding this address to your contacts can help with deliverability.

If you've checked spam and still don't see it, contact us at hello@leagueofficials.app with your account email and we'll investigate the delivery logs.

The Training tab has rules quizzes for each sport at three levels: Rookie, Skilled and Expert. Pass a level to earn its Knowledge Badge and unlock the next one. If you do not pass, you can try again after 30 minutes.

For League Admins

Go to My Games and tap the + button. Choose Post a Game, then fill in the sport, date, time, location, age group, pay and number of officials. Eligible officials nearby see it on their Home screen.

To load a whole season, tap + and upload a schedule file (.xlsx, .xls or .csv) or import a calendar.

If you're an admin for more than one league, tap the league name in the Home header to open the league switcher. Selecting a different league updates all Home data — games, fill rate, officials, and seasons — to reflect that league. You can switch leagues at any time without signing out.

You do not need to approve anyone. The first eligible official to claim an open game is confirmed at once, and you get a notification. To bring in more officials, go to Find Officials and tap Invite Officials.

After the game starts, open it from My Games and tap Mark as Complete. Rate the official and add a report if something needs recording.

Fill rate is the percentage of your games that are filled or completed. Cancelled games are not counted.

Account & Access

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password and enter your account email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Tap the link, then tap Open LeagueOfficials to set a new password in the app.

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact us at hello@leagueofficials.app.

In the app, go to Profile, then Account Settings, then Delete Account, and confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted right away. If you cannot sign in, email hello@leagueofficials.app from the address on your account with "Delete my account" in the subject line, and we will delete it within 5 business days.

Officials can change it in the app: Profile, then Account Settings, then Email. A confirmation goes to both addresses. League admins: email hello@leagueofficials.app from your current address with the new one.

Try the following steps:

  • Double-check your email address.
  • Use Forgot Password to reset your password if you're unsure.
  • Ensure you're using the latest version of the LeagueOfficials app.
  • Check your internet connection and try again.

If none of these resolve the issue, email us at hello@leagueofficials.app with your account email and a description of the error.
